How It Works — Step by Step

From application to your first sale

1
Done ✓

Apply as a Vendor

You submitted your shop name, description, phone, and bank details. Our team reviews applications within 24 hours and sends you an approval email.

2
After Approval

Access Your Dashboard

Once approved, log in and you'll see your Vendor Dashboard automatically. From there you manage all products, view orders, and track your earnings.

3
Key Action

List Your Products

Click "Add Product" and fill in: name, category, description, price, stock count, sizes, colors, and photos. The more detail you provide, the better your sales. Upload up to 10 photos per product — clear, well-lit images convert best.

Customers Find & Buy

Your products are live on ShopLuxe. Customers browse, add to cart, and pay securely via Paystack. You get notified by email when an order comes in. Your dashboard shows all incoming orders instantly.

When a paid order arrives, package the item and arrange delivery to the customer. Once shipped, go to your Dashboard → Orders and click "Mark Shipped". The customer gets notified automatically.

After the order is delivered and confirmed, ShopLuxe processes your payout to the bank account you provided. You keep 90% of the sale price — 10% is ShopLuxe's platform fee.

Adding & Managing Products

What each field means and how to fill it in

  • 🏷️
    Product NameUse a clear, descriptive name. Include brand, model, and key detail — e.g. "Nike Air Force 1 Low White" beats "White Sneaker". Customers search by name.
  • 📂
    CategoryPick the most relevant category (Men, Women, Sneakers, Appliances, etc.). This controls which filtered page your product appears on. You can also type a custom category.
  • 💬
    DescriptionWrite at least 2–3 sentences. Mention material, style, what makes it special, and care instructions. Longer, honest descriptions reduce returns and boost trust.
  • 💵
    Price & Sale PriceSet your regular price. If you want to run a promotion, enter a Sale Price and toggle "On Sale" — customers will see the old price crossed out. Sale Price must be lower than regular price.
  • 📊
    Stock CountEnter the exact quantity you have available. When a customer buys, stock drops automatically. If it hits zero, the product shows "Out of Stock" and blocks new purchases until you restock.
  • 📏
    SizesClick the preset buttons (36–45, S–XXL) or type your own comma-separated sizes. For non-clothing items (appliances, books, etc.) switch to "Dimensions" mode and enter Length × Width × Depth in cm.
  • 🎨
    ColorsClick the color swatches or type color names separated by commas. This helps customers filter and know exactly what variants are available before buying.
  • 📸
    PhotosUpload multiple high-quality images. Click any thumbnail to set the main (cover) photo. Use natural lighting, white or clean backgrounds. Show the product from different angles. Photos are the #1 factor in purchase decisions.
  • ⭐
    Featured & New Arrival toggles"New Arrival" auto-expires after 30 days. "Featured" shows your product in the homepage carousel — use this for your best sellers. These are managed by you but may be adjusted by ShopLuxe admins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common vendor questions answered

Can I list products from any category? ▼
Yes — ShopLuxe supports shoes, clothing, appliances, software, books, accessories, and more. If your category isn't in the dropdown, use the "Other (custom)" option when adding a product.
How do I update my bank details? ▼
Currently bank details are set at application time. To update them, contact ShopLuxe support via the Support page and we'll update your payout account manually.
What happens if a customer wants a refund? ▼
Contact ShopLuxe support. We review refund requests case by case. If the product was as described and properly shipped, refunds are typically not issued. If it was misrepresented, the vendor may bear the cost.
Can I have multiple shops / vendor accounts? ▼
One vendor account per user account. If you need to sell under a different brand, contact support. Creating duplicate accounts to bypass policy will result in a ban.
How do I delete a product? ▼
Go to your Vendor Dashboard → find the product → click Edit → scroll to the bottom and click "Delete Product". This is permanent. If you just want to hide it temporarily, set stock to 0 instead.
Who handles delivery / logistics? ▼
You do. ShopLuxe is the marketplace — you are responsible for packaging and arranging delivery to the customer. Coordinate directly with the customer if needed, using the contact details in the order.
